2. What Antiquo is — and what it is not

Antiquo gives an opinion produced by an AI model from photographs. It is not a professional appraisal, an authentication, or advice on which to buy, sell or insure anything.

Identifying antiques properly requires physical examination by a qualified person. A photograph cannot show weight, sound, tool marks, the underside of a joint, or a mark that is worn away. The App may be wrong, and it may be confidently wrong.

Where no maker's mark is visible in your photographs, the App deliberately leaves the maker blank rather than guessing. Treat a blank field as "unknown", not as "unmarked".

3. About the prices we show

The price range shown is what sellers are currently asking on eBay for items the App judged comparable. It is not a valuation, not a market value, and not a record of what anything actually sold for.

Asking prices are frequently far above what an item realises. Where too few comparable listings exist, the App shows no range at all rather than an unreliable one.

Do not rely on these figures for a purchase, a sale, an insurance declaration or a tax filing.
